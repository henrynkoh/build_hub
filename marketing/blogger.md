## Body

Planning a construction project usually means working across scattered notes, spreadsheets, supplier pages, and schedules. Construction Project Hub is designed to bring the main planning pieces into one organized reference.

## What Is Inside?

- **15 construction workflow steps** showing the general order of work
- **69 material lines** connected to those stages
- **Material and labor planning** with quantities, estimated prices, and labor hours
- **180 Amazon product picks** for beginning a tool and material comparison
- **Affiliate campaign tools** for construction-content creators
- **A 60-video Shorts Studio** for short-form construction content

## Why the Workflow View Helps

A sequence makes it easier to ask practical questions:

- What must be completed before the next stage starts?
- When should materials arrive?
- Which trade or contractor is needed?
- What information must be confirmed with an inspector, engineer, designer, or supplier?

The hub does not replace professional plans. It gives builders and students a structured starting point for discussion and research.

## Who Is It For?

### DIY homebuilders

Use the 15 steps to understand the broad construction sequence before diving into details.

### Contractors and tradespeople

Use the project as a quick reference or as a visual way to explain the sequence to clients and students.

### Construction students

Trace how materials, tools, labor, and workflow stages connect.

### Tool and material shoppers

Use the product lists to begin comparing options. Verify current specifications, availability, reviews, and prices before purchasing.

## A Simple Way to Use the Hub

1. Review the full 15-step workflow.
2. Open the page for your current construction stage.
3. Review the material and labor lines.
4. Adjust quantities and assumptions for your actual project.
5. Compare product options.
6. Confirm everything against local codes, permits, plans, and professional guidance.
