## Safety Note

This content is general information, not professional, engineering, legal, or code-compliance advice. Follow local requirements and use licensed professionals where required.

## Messaging Rules

1. Do not promise savings, profits, faster completion, code compliance, or project outcomes.
2. Do not claim a product is the best or cheapest without current evidence.
3. Describe product picks as starting points for comparison.
4. Include an affiliate disclosure when Amazon links or products are mentioned.
5. Use genuine screenshots and footage. Do not fabricate testimonials, prices, or results.
