## The 15-Step Workflow

The workflow presents the broad order of construction, from site preparation through final punch list and landscaping. This makes it easier to see how one stage depends on another and when materials, equipment, inspections, or trades may be needed.

## Materials and Labor

Each step connects to material and labor data. The site displays quantities, units, planning prices, labor hours, and suppliers. These values help create a first draft, but they must be adjusted for the project's design, location, market, and code requirements.
